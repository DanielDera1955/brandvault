Brand Guidelines
Rules and standards for consistent brand application across all channels
🎨
Logo Usage
When and how to apply the logo
Always maintain clear space equal to the height of the "B" mark on all sides of the logo.
Use the primary dark logo on light backgrounds; use the reversed white logo on dark or image backgrounds.
Never stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects to the logo mark.
Minimum size: 80px wide for digital, 25mm for print.
🎯
Color Application
Palette usage hierarchy
Brand Ink (#1A1A2E) is the dominant color for all primary text and hero backgrounds.
Brand Gold (#C9A84C) is reserved for calls-to-action, highlights, and key accents only.
Never use more than three palette colors in a single composition.
Maintain minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for all body text (WCAG AA).
✍️
Typography Rules
Type hierarchy and pairing
Playfair Display is used exclusively for headings, hero text, and display contexts.
DM Sans is used for all body copy, UI elements, labels, and captions.
Never mix more than two typefaces in any single design asset.
Minimum body text size: 14px digital, 10pt print.
🗣️
Tone of Voice
How we communicate
Confident but not arrogant — state things clearly without over-claiming.
Warm and direct — human, approachable, respectful of the reader's time.
Avoid jargon. If a simpler word exists, use it.
Active voice in all marketing copy. Passive voice only in legal or technical contexts.
📐
Layout & Spacing
Grid and proportions
Use an 8px base unit for all spacing decisions. Margins must be multiples of 8.
Maximum content width: 1200px. Never allow content to span the full viewport width without constraint.
Use 12-column grid for desktop, 4-column for mobile. Always maintain 16px gutter minimum.
Border radius standard: 10px for cards, 6px for form elements, 20px for pills and tags.
🚫
Brand Don'ts
What to always avoid
Do not use the logo on backgrounds that reduce contrast below the minimum threshold.
Do not alter the proportions, weight, or spacing of the wordmark under any circumstance.
Do not apply drop shadows, gradients, or decorative effects directly to the logo.
Do not use unapproved typefaces or colors in any official brand communications.
